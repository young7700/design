Colour

lg.com/uk stages products on near-black grounds and spends colour on exactly one thing: the call to action. The palette below keeps that discipline — a single accent, a single secondary "signal" for promotions, and neutrals with a faint warm bias rather than flat grey.

Ink#0B0B0DProduct-staging background. The black behind hero imagery and the dark-theme ground.
Paper#FAF8F6Documentation ground — warm off-white, never stark white.
Signal Red#A50034The one brand action colour. CTAs, active nav state, the single key badge per view.
Ember#FF3E63Red lifted for AA contrast on Ink — the accent's dark-mode voice, same hue family.
Slate#6B6670Secondary text, captions, and the hue borders are mixed from — never pure grey.
Amber Signal#C97A2BPromotional badges — NEW, SALE, %OFF. Reserved strictly for merchandising, never actions.

Guidelines

The rules that keep the system feeling like one product, drawn from how lg.com/uk actually uses restraint.

Do

  • ✓Use Signal Red for exactly one action per screen.
  • ✓Stage product imagery on Ink or a gradient, never a flat mid-tone.
  • ✓Set badges in Plex Mono so they read as data, not decoration.
  • ✓Keep the primary nav dark even on a light-theme page.

Don't

  • ✕Pair two accent buttons in the same view.
  • ✕Use Amber Signal for anything but merchandising badges.
  • ✕Round card corners more than 10px — the brand reads square.
  • ✕Let body copy run past ~65 characters per line.
